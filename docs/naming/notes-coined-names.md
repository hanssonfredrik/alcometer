Method note: domain status was checked live through registry RDAP (Verisign for .com, Google Registry for .app). HTTP 200 means registered and 404 means no registration record, which most likely means available. 429 means rate-limited, so status unknown. Registered domains were then probed over HTTPS to see whether a site is live. Store presence was checked with web searches against App Store, Google Play and aggregator listings, not inside the store apps. **Trademark databases could not be queried.** The USPTO tmsearch API rejected scripted requests (HTTP 405), and EUIPO eSearch, TMview and PRV are JS-only interfaces with no tool access in this session. No trademark result below counts as clearance.

Candidates proposed (15): Ebb, Ebbly, Ebbglow, Ebbtide, Afterglow, Nightglow, Glowtide, Glint, Lumo, Tidely, Sippa, Glimra, Nocto, Noctil, Promilo.

## Q1. Do apps with these or confusingly similar names exist on the App Store or Google Play?

### Takeaway
Real, short words (Ebb, Lumo, Glint, Tidely, Sippa, Nocto, Glimra) are already taken by apps, several of them in health or wellness. Coined compounds (Ebbly, Ebbglow, Glowtide, Noctil) returned no app hits. Nightglow and Promilo have hits only in unrelated categories.

### Cited Findings
- **Ebb**: Headspace ships an AI companion called "Ebb" inside its mental-health app (US/UK/CA/AU). That is a strong wellness-category conflict. — [Headspace](https://www.headspace.com/ebb); [BusinessWire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241010397470/en/Mental-Health-Company-Headspace-Launches-Empathetic-AI-Companion)
- **Ebb**: there is also "Ebb: Pelvic Floor Release", a paid Health & Fitness iOS app. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6778668329)
- **Ebb**: an "Ebb" period tracker is on F-Droid. — [F-Droid](https://f-droid.org/es/packages/com.superdavelab.ebb/)
- **Ebb**: "Ebb – Evaluate. Breathe. Begin." is a stress app for Apple Watch. — [iPhoneAddict](https://iphoneaddict.fr/apps/forme-sante/ebb-evaluate-breathe-begin.html)
- **Ebbly**: a web search found no app with this name. The closest hits were "Ebb" apps and the unrelated "eb:fly". — [search results incl. DocCheck](https://www.doccheck.com/de/detail/articles/51158-wie-revolutioniert-die-neue-app-eb-fly-die-versorgung-von-epidermolysis-bullosa)
- **Ebbtide**: "Ebb Tide – Free Tides" (iOS) and "Ebb and Flow Tide Clock" exist. Both are tide apps, so a different category. — [AppFollow](https://appfollow.io/ios/ebb-tide-free-tides/999963801?country=us); [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6443869696)
- **Glowtide / Ebbglow**: no apps found. — same search as above ([AppFollow](https://appfollow.io/ios/ebb-tide-free-tides/999963801?country=us) was the closest hit)
- **Afterglow**: "Afterglow – memory diary" is a Lifestyle iOS app with very few ratings. AF Drinks uses **Afterglow™** for a botanical extract in non-alcoholic drinks that mimics alcohol's warmth. That puts the mark directly in the drinks space. — [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6741039140); [AF Drinks](https://www.af-drinks.com/pages/afterglow)
- **Nightglow**: "NightGlow: Selfie Light Cam" (iOS, Photo & Video) and a "NightGlow – Bedroom Light" Android night-light app exist. ### Inferences
- Cut for store conflicts: Ebb (Headspace), Sippa (near-identical drink-logging and streak concept), Lumo (Proton's privacy brand), Glint and Tidely (crowded), Glimra (skin app launching, plus a Swedish trade name).
- Ebbtide is weak because tide apps are established and ebbtide.app is in use.
- Afterglow is cut: "Afterglow™" is already used in the beverage sector by AF Drinks, and the word glamorises the after-drinking feeling.

### Gaps
- I did not search inside the App Store or Play Store apps themselves (regional storefronts, especially the Swedish one, may differ). Small or new apps may not be indexed by web search.

## Q2. Is there an obvious trademark conflict (EUIPO/TMview, USPTO, PRV; classes 9/42/44)?

### Takeaway
Not verified for any candidate. Programmatic access failed and the registers have no tool-accessible interface. The only clear risks found are common-law and brand risks: Headspace "Ebb", AF Drinks "Afterglow™", Proton "Lumo", and the Glimra car-wash and skin brands.

### Cited Findings
- AF Drinks marks "Afterglow" with ™ for a drink ingredient, so it is claimed as a mark in the beverage sector. — [AF Drinks](https://www.af-drinks.com/pages/afterglow)
- A general web search for "Afterglow" in class 9 returned only class-guide pages, no register records. — [Gerben Law](https://www.gerbenlaw.com/?p=81)
- Downloadable apps fall under class 9 and online or SaaS software under class 42, so both should be searched. — [Gerben Law](https://www.gerbenlaw.com/?p=81); [Patron Accounting](https://www.patronaccounting.com/blog/trademark-for-software-and-apps-class-9-and-42)
- USPTO tmsearch API: a scripted POST returned HTTP 405 MethodNotAllowed for every term (ebbly, ebbglow, nightglow, glimra, nocto, sippa, tidely, afterglow, noctil, promilo). I recorded this directly; there is no URL to cite.

### Inferences
- Coined forms (Ebbly, Ebbglow, Noctil, Glowtide) are inherently more distinctive and less likely to collide than dictionary words. They still need a manual check, especially for "EBB"-prefixed marks in classes 9, 42 and 44 because of Headspace.

### Gaps
- No search was done in EUIPO eSearch, TMview, USPTO or PRV. Before committing, do a manual check in tmview.org (covers EU, PRV and other offices), tmsearch.uspto.gov and the PRV Varumärkesdatabasen for classes 9, 42 and 44.

## Q4. Meaning, pronunciation, connotations (EN/SV) and risks: survivors

### Takeaway
Six candidates survive: **Ebbly, Ebbglow, Noctil, Nightglow, Glowtide, Promilo**. The strongest are Ebbly and Ebbglow, because "ebb" describes BAC fading and the word means the same in Swedish ("ebb och flod"). Promilo carries a driving-limit association in Sweden.

### Cited Findings
- No app or trademark-like use was found for Ebbly, Ebbglow, Glowtide or Noctil (see Q1 sources).
- Noctiluca is the bioluminescent "sea sparkle" plankton name used by a lighting studio, which fits the neon glow idea. — [Lightzoomlumière](https://www.lightzoomlumiere.fr/sur/noctiluca/)

### Inferences (linguistic judgments; no source found, so treat as my assessment)
1. **Ebbly** ("EB-lee")
   - Meaning: "ebb" means to recede, a metaphor for BAC decaying and for drinking less.
   - Swedish: "ebb" is the same word, so it reads cleanly.
   - Strengths: spelling is easy and it is short and friendly.
   - Risks: ebbly.com is held, and Headspace "Ebb" is in the wellness space (a different form, but the same root).
   - Domains: getebbly.app is open.
2. **Ebbglow** ("EB-glow")
   - Meaning: the glow ebbs, a direct neon metaphor for a fading level.
   - Swedish: no negative meaning.
   - Strengths: ebbglow.com and ebbglow.app are both unregistered, and no store hits were found.
   - Risks: the double consonant ("bbg") is slightly awkward to type.
3. **Noctil** ("NOK-til")
   - Meaning: from noct- (night) and noctiluca (glowing plankton), giving a night-and-glow feel.
   - Strengths: noctil.app is open and no app hits were found.
   - Risks: it is less self-explanatory, Swedes may say it "nok-TEEL", and it is close to Petzl NOCTILIGHT (a physical product, low risk).
4. **Nightglow**
   - Meaning: a real English word (airglow at night), with a strong neon fit.
   - Swedish: Swedes will understand it.
   - Strengths: nightglow.app is open.
   - Risks: existing NightGlow selfie-light and night-light apps in other categories. It also reads like "glowing at night out", which may glamorise drinking, and it is less distinctive.
5. **Glowtide**
   - Meaning: glow plus tide, a level that rises and falls.
   - Swedish: no negative meaning.
   - Strengths: no app hits.
   - Risks: .com and .app are both registered and for sale on Spaceship, so they would have to be bought.
6. **Promilo** ("pro-MEE-lo")
   - Meaning: a play on promille/‰, the exact unit the app shows.
   - Swedish: Swedes will instantly connect it to "promille".
   - Strengths: promilo.app is open.
   - Risks: in Sweden "promille" is strongly associated with drunk-driving limits, which conflicts with the "never imply fitness to drive" rule, so marketing must avoid any limit framing. promilo.com is an unrelated live edtech site.

Cut, with the main reason:
- Ebb: Headspace and multiple wellness apps.
- Afterglow: AF Drinks ™ and glamorisation.
- Sippa: a near-identical hydration and streak app.
- Lumo: Proton.
- Glint: crowded, and also Microsoft Glint, from general knowledge, unverified.
- Tidely: crowded.
- Glimra: skin app plus a Swedish car-wash brand.
- Nocto: live app and domain.
- Ebbtide: tide apps.

### Gaps
- Pronunciation and connotation judgments have not been tested with Swedish or English speakers.
- The "promille → driving" association in Sweden is my inference and was not sourced.
- Trademark clearance is still outstanding for every survivor (see Q2).
