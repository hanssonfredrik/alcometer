About the estimate

A rough guide, not a measurement.

The level is an estimate from the Widmark formula. It uses your weight, your sex and the time since your first drink. Your real level can be quite different, depending on food, health and medication.

Never use it to decide whether you can drive.

Questions

Common questions

What is Promilla?

Promilla is a private drink journal for your phone and the web. You log what you drink, see an estimate of your evening and build streaks of alcohol-free days. Everything stays on your phone.

Is Promilla free?

Yes. Promilla is free, with no ads and no in-app purchases.

Does Promilla collect my data?

No. There is no account, no tracking and no server. Your drinks are saved on your phone. You can save a backup file or export them as CSV.

How does Promilla estimate my level?

It uses the Widmark formula with your weight and sex. Each drink raises the estimate, and it falls by 0.15 ‰ an hour until it reaches zero. How the estimate works shows the full math.

Can I use Promilla to decide whether I can drive?

No. The level is a rough estimate, and your real level can be quite different. Never use it to decide whether you can drive.

What is a standard drink?

A standard drink is 12 g of pure alcohol. A 33 cl beer at 5% is about 1.1 standard drinks. Promilla counts your week in standard drinks against a weekly target you set.

What counts as an alcohol-free day?

A day with no drinks logged. Days roll over at 05:00, so a drink at 01:00 counts toward the evening before.

Which phones does Promilla work on?

Android through Google Play, iPhone through the App Store (coming soon) and any modern browser at app.promilla.se. The web app works offline once it has loaded.
