Promilla

Privacy and support

Effective 20 October 2026 · applies to the web app and the iOS and Android apps
Promilla collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the app never sends your data anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What is stored, and where

The app stores only what you enter: the drinks you log, your weight and sex (used for the estimate), your weekly target, your usual drink sizes, each day's limit and the color you picked.

  • In the web app, it lives in your browser's storage for this site.
  • On iOS and Android, it lives in the app's private storage on your phone.

Nothing is sent to a server. We run no backend and hold no copy of your data, so we couldn't access, share or sell it even if asked.

Your data leaves the app only when you choose to: when you save a backup file or export your drinks as CSV, the file goes wherever you save or share it.

Deleting your data

  • iOS / Android: uninstall the app. All data is removed with it.
  • Web: clear this site's browsing data in your browser settings.

There is nothing to delete anywhere else. No copy exists on any server.

Third parties

None. The app has no third-party analytics, advertising or social code, and it makes no network requests with your data.

Health disclaimer

The blood-alcohol level the app shows is an estimate from the Widmark formula. Your real level can be quite different. Never use it to decide whether you can drive, operate machinery or do anything else where alcohol matters. If in doubt, don't.
